# Build Words! - PH Words for Bobo

A word-building game where Bobo learns to spell words containing the "PH" digraph. Each word is illustrated with a procedurally-drawn picture, and the player picks letters in order to spell the word while avoiding distractor letters.

![Title Screen](screenshot.png)

## Word List

PHONE, GRAPH, SPHERE, PHEW, NEPHEW, MORPH, PHONICS, TYPHOON, PHRASE, PHASE, GOPHER, DOLPHIN, PHOTO, PHARMACY, ELEPHANT

## How to Play

1. A picture appears with the word spoken aloud
2. Letter tiles are scattered around the picture — tap/click the correct letters in order to spell the word
3. Wrong letters shake gently (no penalty)
4. Complete all 15 words to win!

## Controls

- **Desktop**: Click letter tiles or type on keyboard
- **iPad**: Tap letter tiles

## Running

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve for iPad:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open `http://<mac_ip>:8080/ipad.html` on iPad.
